MettleRNASeq — comparison with archived MSc/article supplements
9 October 2026 · development version 1.0.0.9000

Conclusion for the checked outputs
No substantive numerical change was found in the checked bone and skin outputs.
Differences at floating-point precision are recorded in the comparison CSV.
The default ICC-difference table has fewer rows because it now lists each
undirected pair once and excludes the diagonal; the coefficients are unchanged.
This comparison does not certify every result or scientific conclusion in the
dissertation/article, and it is not independent biological validation.

Sources and matching
The reference was the archived Supplementary Material folder, including its
raw script, count matrices, PCA outputs, resample indices, classification scores,
gene-ID/symbol mapping, correlations and rule tables. Numerical matrices and
tables were matched by identifiers; incidental leading spaces in gene symbols
were removed for matching, without changing numerical values.

What matched
1. TMM normalization of all 12 samples: 21,590 bone genes and 21,197 skin genes.
   Settings match the raw script: min_count=10, group_size=4 and
   remove_low_variance=FALSE. A separate check found no retained zero-variance
   genes in either raw matrix; the default variance filter therefore removes
   no additional genes in these inputs.
2. Every archived Euclidean sample distance and PCA eigenvalue/variance output
   for three conditions and for the FLASH/STANDARD subset.
3. Four-PC, 100-genes-per-PC selection: the same 235 bone and 186 skin gene IDs,
   with matching expression values.
4. All 100,000 archived gene resamples across sizes 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 in both
   tissues: 3,000,000 sampled positions matched seed=123 draws.
5. All 400,000 stored classification partition-score rows were checked for
   consistency between their recorded summaries and the median of their stored
   test accuracies. This is an archive-consistency check, not a refit of those models.
6. The historical selected-resample unions reproduced 50 bone and 18 skin genes
   used in the correlation outputs. These historical resample IDs were supplied;
   the complete model search selecting those IDs was not rerun.
7. Every ACC coefficient, both ICC matrices and every absolute ICC difference.
8. Rule comparison using the archived mined-rule tables: bone has 164 shared,
   432 FLASH-only and 312 STANDARD-only gene-set rules; skin has 10 shared,
   38 FLASH-only and 46 STANDARD-only rules. All these sets matched.
9. Eight RF partition fits were reconstructed from the raw supplementary
   function and compared with the corrected package and archived values:
   first resample, 10 genes, four partitions per tissue. Training and test
   accuracies matched in all eight. The raw script's automatic tuning grid was
   reproduced explicitly for the package; arbitrary tutorial grids are different choices.

The confirmed presentation change
Bone: 2,500 historical difference-table rows become 1,225 unique off-diagonal pairs.
Skin: 324 historical rows become 153 unique off-diagonal pairs.
icc_difference(..., unique_pairs=FALSE) reproduces the historical full-table
representation, with matching pairwise values. Row ordering can also differ.

Why the rule-comparison fix did not change these archived sets
Every archived retained rule in the four condition/tissue tables has a single
antecedent item. The older expression-stripping code could lose additional
antecedent genes in longer rules, but that case is absent from these retained
tables. The comparison performed on these archived rule tables matched exactly.
This does not establish compatibility for arbitrary future multi-item rules.

What was not fully rerun
The complete 400,000-fit RF search, all other classifiers/benchmarks,
differential-expression analyses (DESeq2, edgeR, limma), enrichment analyses,
and complete Apriori/FP-growth mining runs were not reexecuted. The mined-rule
tables were preserved and used as comparison inputs; matching their comparison
does not prove full mining reproducibility. Graph layout and cluster numbering
were not compared. Historical source files were not edited or overwritten.

Evidence files
supplement_comparison.csv: stage-by-stage numerical and set comparisons.
archived_rf_comparison.csv: all eight reconstructed fit scores.
supplement_evidence.json: counts, constant-gene checks and retained-rule structure.
The associated R/Python scripts and logs document the calculations.
